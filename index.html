Why Natural > Fossil

  • Infinite supply. The sun delivers more energy in 1 hour than humanity uses in a year.
  • Clean air. No SO₂, NOₓ, or particulates — cuts respiratory illness and premature deaths.
  • Stable climate. Slashes greenhouse gases that drive warming, sea level rise, and extreme weather.
  • Cheaper now. Utility-scale solar and onshore wind are the cheapest new electricity sources on Earth.
  • Water-friendly. Wind and solar PV use ~0 water vs thermal plants that consume billions of gallons.
  • Energy independence. Sun and wind aren't imported, embargoed, or speculated on.
